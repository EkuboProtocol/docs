# Ve33

Ve33 is an [extension](../concepts/extensions.md) on Ekubo Core that turns liquidity incentives into a marketplace governed by a **stake token**. Holders lock the token, decide which pools receive emissions, and set the fees those pools charge. Voters earn the trading fees generated by the pools they support, giving them a direct financial stake in how rewards are allocated.

Anyone can deploy a Ve33 instance around a token of their choice — including an existing token. The chosen token is both the asset locked for voting power and the reward earned by liquidity providers. The extension is permissionless and ownerless: there is no admin, no treasury share, no weekly epoch, and the emission policy lives outside the base extension.

The design draws on the vote-escrow lineage of Curve's veCRV and Solidly's ve(3,3) (later refined by Velodrome and Aerodrome), with one key addition: **voters set the pool fee** as well as directing emissions. Read the full essay for the history and economics: [Ve33: a token-governed liquidity marketplace](https://hackmd.io/@moodysalem/r1VnZQr4fg).

## How it works

* **Locking**: holders lock the stake token for up to 4 years. Voting power is linear:

  ```
  voting power = stake amount × time remaining / 4 years
  ```

  The [`VeToken`](https://github.com/EkuboProtocol/evm-contracts/blob/main/src/VeToken.sol) wrapper represents a lock as a transferable ERC‑721. Stakes can be added to, extended, split into several positions, or merged.
* **Voting**: a stake assigns all of its voting power to one pool (split your stake to support several). A vote specifies two things: the pool that should receive a share of global LP emissions, and the **swap fee** that pool should charge. The active pool fee is the voting-power-weighted average of all fee votes; a pool with no active vote weight charges a zero extension fee.
* **Fees → voters**: the full pool fee accrues to the voters supporting that pool, in proportion to their recorded vote weight.
* **Emissions → LPs**: funded emissions of the stake token stream to active liquidity in each pool, in proportion to the pool's vote weight. Rewards are range-aware for concentrated positions (only in-range liquidity earns) and global for stableswap pools.
* **Continuous accounting**: unlike weekly-epoch ve(3,3) systems, fees and emissions accrue continuously through on-chain growth indices, updated whenever a pool or position is touched. There is no weekly distribution transaction. Note that vote weights are *sampled* — stored pool weights only change when a vote is cast or refreshed, so stake owners should renew votes as locks approach expiration.
* **Emission schedules are modular**: anyone can fund a schedule for a chosen interval, and an optional scheduler can maintain a governance-configured target rate for a mintable token.

Every Ve33 pool runs on Ekubo Core: the extension contains only incentive and accounting logic, while Core supplies swap execution, concentrated liquidity and stableswap math, singleton settlement, and the shared router/integration surface. Swaps on Ve33 pools must be forwarded through a Ve33-aware router (direct Core swaps revert, since the voter-selected fee is applied by the extension).

## STONX on Robinhood Chain

The first ecosystem deployment of Ve33 is **STONX** on [Robinhood Chain](https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/robinhood-chain-launches-public-testnet), coordinating liquidity across stock-token markets. As proposed ([STONX on Robinhood Chain](https://hackmd.io/@moodysalem/stonx-on-rhc)):

* One **STONX** stake token directs rewards across multiple stock-token pools
* DAO-owned STONX/USDG liquidity
* 100 days of initially funded emissions, with the initial vote direction toward stock-token markets
* Ekubo, Inc.'s allocation max-staked, with its pool-fee income funding continued development

STONX voters continuously answer the marketplace's core questions with their votes: which stock-token markets receive emissions, what fee gives traders competitive execution while producing useful voter revenue, and whether the volume that follows each unit of emissions justifies the issuance.
