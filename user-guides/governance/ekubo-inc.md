# Ekubo, Inc. and the DAO

Ekubo, Inc. is the Delaware C corporation that built the initial version of Ekubo Protocol, along with the [indexer](https://github.com/ekuboprotocol/indexer), the [interface](https://app.ekubo.org/), the [governance contracts](https://github.com/EkuboProtocol/governance), and the [API](../../reference/ekubo-api/README.md). It was founded by [Moody Salem](https://x.com/sendmoodz).

Ekubo, Inc. bootstrapped the Ekubo DAO in May 2024, which quickly became an exemplar of decentralized productivity: over 95% of total supply distributed, 60% staked, and 8 proposals passed in its first 2 months. The Ekubo DAO received the largest [Starknet Catalyst Program grant](https://www.starknet.io/blog/announcing-the-catalyst-program-igniting-transformative-change/) in recognition of the effort to further the state of the art for protocol development, token distribution and decentralized governance.

## The company's role

In July 2024, the DAO approved a proposal defining Ekubo, Inc.'s role, in exchange for a one-time grant of ~$1.5M in ETH, STRK, and USDC — intended to be the only grant the company ever requests from the DAO. Under it, Ekubo, Inc. committed to:

* Develop the Ekubo Protocol core contracts for the benefit of the DAO, including Cairo version updates, an on-chain limit order extension, and making source code available at the direction of the DAO (license and timeline decided by the DAO)
* Design and implement a framework for distributing protocol revenue to EKUBO stakers at regular intervals (delivered as [revenue buybacks](../../reference/contracts/starknet.md))
* Develop and host the [Ekubo interface](https://app.ekubo.org), free to use for swaps, with a public feature prioritization process (the interface is proprietary and owned solely by Ekubo, Inc.)
* Maintain the documentation at docs.ekubo.org
* Provide developer support in the [Discord](https://discord.ekubo.org) and technical support to DAO delegates for creating proposals
* Operate and make freely available Ekubo data via the [public API](../../reference/ekubo-api/README.md), with rate limits adjusted to keep operational expenses in check
* Open source and maintain the governance tooling under a permissive license

Ekubo, Inc. holds one third of the total EKUBO supply and has committed to **never selling** those tokens for as long as the company exists, keeping the company permanently incentive-aligned with the protocol.

## Areas for ecosystem contribution

Ekubo, Inc. deliberately does not focus on everything. The protocol benefits from ecosystem participation in areas such as:

* Liquidity provider tooling — advanced analytics and automated liquidity management
* Advanced delegate and governance tooling
* Market analytics (e.g. Dune dashboards)
* Aggregator and advanced routing integrations
* Marketing, community management, hackathons, and user guides
* Centralized exchange listings and other token integrations

If you want to build in one of these areas, reach out in the [Discord](https://discord.ekubo.org).
