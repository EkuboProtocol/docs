# Roadmap

Ekubo Protocol is currently live on Starknet Goerli, and you can create testnet positions by connecting a wallet to [app.ekubo.org](https://app.ekubo.org).

Starknet is a rapidly evolving platform, and our priorities may change as we learn more, but we expect to reach the following milestones in the coming months:

* Mainnet closed-beta launch: End of August 2023
  * Audit completed: End of September 2023
  * Aggregators live: August/September 2023
* Limit orders: End of September 2023
* TWAMM orders: EOY 2023
* Volition deployment and migration: EOY 2023

Last updated August 2023
