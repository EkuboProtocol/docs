# Roadmap

Ekubo Protocol is currently live on Starknet, and you can create positions by connecting a wallet to [app.ekubo.org](https://app.ekubo.org).

Starknet is a rapidly evolving platform, and our priorities may change as we learn more, but we expect to reach the following milestones in the coming months:

* ✅ Mainnet closed-beta launch: End of August 2023
  * Audit completion has been postponed to Q4 2023
    * During this period, Ekubo is **unaudited**. Use at your own risk!
  * ✅ Aggregators live: August/September 2023
    * [**AVNU**](https://app.avnu.fi) live 9/14/23
    * [**Fibrous**](https://app.fibrous.finance/) live 9/24/23
* Limit orders: Late October 2023
* TWAMM orders: Q1 2024
* Volition: Q2 2024
